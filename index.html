What is KIPP

From grind to card in 3 steps.

  1. 01

    Link your account

    You connect your account yourself, voluntarily. Planned via Riot Sign-On, subject to Riot's approval. Only then is your form "verified."

    Planned for the beta

  2. 02

    Your card evolves with you

    Form score, form trend, player archetype and Moment Cards after strong games. Measured relative to your rank and role.

    Core of the beta

  3. 03

    Share it and get signed

    You share your card. Managers sign you to their team and go head to head in the Weekly Cup and mini-leagues.

    Manager layer planned

For players

Your form becomes a contract.

Whether you're grinding in Emerald or climbing through Platinum: you don't just show where you stand, but where you're headed.

  • A contract that grows with you

    Its value is tied to your verified form, not bought advantages, with a breakdown of why you rank where you do. One season per contract — after that you're a free agent again.

  • Your contract pays out per win

    Each match your team wins pays out a slice of your own contract — scaled by your form that game and your team's Aura. Virtual points for now, no real payments yet.

  • Promo: when you rank up, your card does too

    A rank-up earns its own promo card. Sharing, download and link preview stay free. Always.

  • Be seen, on your terms

    A public player page only exists with your opt-in. You can switch it off anytime. Your own card is fully usable even without a single manager.

For sponsors Pilot planned

Don't back some logo slot. Back a player.

With KIPP, brands are meant to back individual players and become part of their journey — right where the community is already looking: on the card.

The pilot starts only once the core loop is proven — at first with physical prizes like peripherals or energy drinks. Backing reaches the player as growth support and clearly-labeled brand presence on their card. No real payments yet.

Register sponsor interest

What sponsors are meant to get

  • A patch on the backed player's card and their shared Moment Cards, clearly labeled as advertising
  • Measurable reach from shared cards instead of gut feeling
  • A genuine development story across an entire season

What sponsors never get

  • Any influence over form score, ranking or scout status
  • Access to player data being shared with them
  • Returns, shares or dividends on players

We only announce prizes and pools once a sponsor has committed to them contractually. Right now there are no committed sponsors and no prizes.

Is KIPP for you?

Who KIPP is for.

KIPP fits anyone who already grinds League of Legends ranked and wants it to count for something — and anyone with an eye for talent who wants to build a team.

For players

A great fit if you…

  • grind ranked solo/duo or flex and want your form to become an identity, not just a number
  • are Platinum+/Emerald+ (the DACH beta focus) — though any rank can build a card
  • want to get scouted and signed to a 5-stack
  • play with the same friends — your Team Aura rewards a stable, in-sync roster

What you get

  • A verified form card that evolves with every game — form score, trend and player archetype, measured against your rank and role
  • Promo cards on rank-up and Moment Cards after standout games — free, and always yours to share
  • Contract payouts per win, scaled by your form that game — virtual points for now, no real payments yet
  • Visibility on your terms — a public page only with your opt-in, and a card that works even with zero managers

For managers & scouts

A great fit if you…

  • have an eye for talent and want to prove your read — no ranked form of your own needed
  • are a teammate, coach, or just love the manager / fantasy meta
  • want to build and run a 5-stack and climb leagues & cups

What you get

  • Scout risers early — every signing is timestamped, so if your player breaks out there's proof you saw it first
  • Points from your 5-stack's Team Form, and Team Aura that grows when the five synergize — a balanced team beats five lone carries
  • A living scouting reputation and head-to-head Weekly Cups & friends' mini-leagues
  • Open, explained scoring — no pay-to-win, ever

Maybe not the right fit (yet) if…

you're under 18, outside the DACH beta focus, expecting cash payouts today (there are no real payments yet), or hoping to buy rank or advantages (that will never exist here). KIPP is a performance and identity product — not an investment and with no pay-to-win.
